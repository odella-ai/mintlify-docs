## Make the request easy to check

Name the **outcome**, **source material**, **constraints**, and **review criteria**. For example:

> Review the attached supplier proposal. Return a one-page summary of cost, delivery dates, missing details, and questions for me. Cite the source for each finding. Do not contact the supplier or change external records.

Instructions guide behavior; configure the worker's actual [access and action policies](/guides/access-and-security) separately.

## Follow the task through review

1. Open **Tasks → New task**, choose the worker, and describe the outcome. [Create and track a task](/workforce/tasks).
2. Follow progress. If the worker asks a question, respond with the missing information rather than starting duplicate work.
3. Inspect any requested action before approving it. Confirm the application, recipients, files, and proposed changes. [Review approvals](/workforce/approvals).
4. Open the returned answer and files. Check accuracy, missing information, and whether the deliverable meets your request.
5. Give feedback and update durable instructions if the same issue is likely to recur.

A completed status is not a substitute for checking the result. Approval of an action is also different from acceptance of the final deliverable.

## When work is stuck

Check the task's question or error first. Then check the worker's availability, required connection, access, approvals, and [account limits](/platform/usage-and-limits). Retrying blindly can duplicate work or external effects.
