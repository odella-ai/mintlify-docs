## Start with a conversation

Open **Workforce**, select your AI Employee, and choose **Chat** where available. Write your request, attach relevant sample material, and wait for uploads to finish before sending. [Chat with an AI Employee](/workforce/chat) covers the full flow.

Prefer email or workplace messaging? Use the worker's published contact details. [Contact an employee through channels](/workforce/contact-an-employee) explains everyday use; the separate [setup directory](/workforce/communication-channels) is for connecting a channel. You can also use [Talk](/workforce/talk) for voice where available in browser chat.

## Make the request easy to check

Name the **outcome**, **source material**, **constraints**, and **review criteria**. For example:

> Review the attached supplier proposal. Return a one-page summary of cost, delivery dates, missing details, and questions for me. Cite the source for each finding. Do not contact the supplier or change external records.

Instructions guide behavior; configure the worker's actual [access and action policies](/guides/access-and-security) separately.

## Work through questions and approvals

1. Follow the reply and any work updates. Answer clarification questions in the relevant conversation rather than starting duplicate work.
2. When the worker requests approval, inspect the application, recipients, files, and proposed changes before deciding. Use the request's controls, not just a typed “yes.”
3. On **Signal**, react to the **approval message** with **👍 to approve once** or **👎 to deny** when offered. Other channels use their own buttons or review links. [Review approvals](/workforce/approvals#approvals-in-each-channel) explains the differences and authorization requirements.
4. If the worker needs a password, MFA, or a CAPTCHA, open its browser handoff and choose **Take over** where offered. Complete the necessary step yourself, then select **I'm done**. Do not put secrets in chat. [Computer and browser handoff](/workforce/computer) also explains saved logins and optional Vault saving.

An approval permits the proposed action; it is not acceptance of the final deliverable. Completing a browser handoff is not blanket authorization for later actions.

## Review the result

Open the returned answer and files. Check accuracy, sources, missing information, and whether the deliverable meets your request. Give corrections in the conversation; update durable instructions if the same issue is likely to recur.

Some requests lead to delegated tasks. Use [Tasks](/workforce/tasks) to inspect their status, blockers, and deliverables, or to create a separate tracked assignment when that suits the work. A completed status is not a substitute for checking the result.

## When work is stuck

Read the latest question, approval, handoff, or error first. Then check the worker's availability, required connection, access, and [account limits](/platform/usage-and-limits). Retrying blindly can duplicate work or external effects.
