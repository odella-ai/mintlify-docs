## Set up your team

- [Invite members and create teams](/platform/organization).
- [Create and assign permission roles](/platform/roles-and-permissions).

Membership, team roles, resource access, and worker editing are separate controls. Use the [team-restricted worker tutorial](/platform/secure-employee-tutorial) for a complete example.

Credits, capacity, availability, and permissions are different checks. A funded account does not guarantee that a particular operation is permitted.

## Maintain shared settings

- [Brand guidelines](/platform/brand-guidelines): provide approved brand context.
- [Models and privacy](/platform/models): review available model choices and account privacy settings.
- [Phone numbers](/platform/phone-numbers): acquire, assign, or release workforce numbers.

## When someone leaves or changes roles

Reassign their workers deliberately, review automations and connections, and remove independent access grants. Follow [Hand over an AI Employee](/platform/manager-handover-tutorial).

Member removal, worker termination, provider-credential revocation, and account deletion are different operations. Review each separately; one does not replace the others.
