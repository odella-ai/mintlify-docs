## Make three separate decisions

1. **Who can use the worker?** Use teams, roles, and the worker's primary audience. [Access control](/platform/access-control).
2. **Who can change its setup?** Assign a manager and selected editing areas in [Governance](/workforce/governance).
3. **What may it do?** Assign only necessary resources and configure [tool, messaging, and approval policies](/workforce/settings-security-privacy).

Instructions alone do not enforce these restrictions. An approval also does not supply a missing resource permission or provider connection.
