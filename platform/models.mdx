# Choose models for the work

Models are one part of how an AI Employee performs its role. Responsibilities, approved source material, tools, instructions, and human review matter just as much as model selection.

## Compare available models

Open **Account → Models** to review the available models, capabilities, and pricing.

- Text models show token costs.
- Image, video, and audio models show their applicable billing units.
- Availability and supported choices can change as models are added or retired.

The Models page is a comparison reference. To change an individual worker's language model, reasoning effort, image/video defaults, or capacity, open its **Settings → Model & Intelligence**. Review any proposed quote change before saving.

See [Worker model settings](/workforce/settings-model-intelligence) and [Billing and credits](/platform/billing-and-credits).

## Zero data retention for text

In an organization account, open **Account → Organization → Settings → Model privacy**. With the appropriate account-settings permission, use **Zero data retention** to restrict AI Employee text traffic to providers that do not retain prompts or outputs.

When enabled:

- Supported text traffic uses eligible providers.
- Unsupported text models fall back to the platform default.
- Image and video generation are unchanged.

<Warning>
  This is a text-provider retention control, not a promise that Odella stores no
  conversation history or that every connected service retains no data. It does
  not configure image/video retention, document-store disposal, or third-party
  integration policies.
</Warning>

If the control is read-only, ask a person with account-settings update permission to review it. After changing it, test representative work and check any change in model behavior.

## Match models to responsibilities

1. Identify the type of work: drafting, extraction, research, analysis, or media creation.
2. Review the sensitivity of the information and your organization's provider/privacy requirements.
3. Choose an available model and reasoning setting appropriate for the task.
4. Test normal, missing-data, and difficult cases against approved review criteria.
5. Compare quality and actual usage before expanding responsibilities.

A more expensive model or higher reasoning setting does not guarantee correct results. Keep a review step for consequential outputs.

## Retired models and defaults

A saved retired model can appear as **Deprecated** until you select a current option. Onboarding can also supply a different configuration from platform defaults. Check the worker's saved settings and quote rather than assuming every worker uses the latest default.

For access and wider data-handling guidance, see [Roles and permissions](/platform/roles-and-permissions) and [Platform security](/platform/security).
