# Give your workforce approved brand context

Open **Account → Organization → Brand** to prepare a shared source of guidance for writing style, visuals, and brand usage. You need permission to update the organization's brand.

## Generate a brand pack

1. Start the brand generation flow from the **Brand** tab.
2. Enter the organization's **Website URL**.
3. Add relevant notes and optional approved evidence, such as logos, decks, screenshots, fonts, or a supported ZIP pack. Follow the uploader's current file limits.
4. Select **Generate** and monitor the background task.
5. Return to the Brand tab to review the result and generation status.

You can continue other work while generation runs. A queued or running task is not yet a completed pack.

## Review the guidance

Check the generated summary and available artifacts against approved source material:

- Brand name and description.
- Writing voice, tone, and terminology.
- Colors, typography, and visual rules where present.
- Unsupported claims, outdated information, or source conflicts.

The generated pack is intended to provide organization brand context in worker workspaces. Still test a representative worker output after changes; available context is not a guarantee of perfect adherence.

## Update or regenerate

Use **Regenerate** with corrected sources or more specific notes when the brand changes or the first result is inaccurate. Review the new result before relying on it for customer-facing work.

Keep [worker instructions](/workforce/identity) and relevant [private skills](/workforce/create-a-skill) consistent with the approved guidance. Avoid contradictory rules in several places.

## Troubleshooting

- **Still queued or running:** Check the task status before submitting another generation.
- **Failed generation:** Review the reported error, website accessibility, and uploaded files, then retry with corrected inputs.
- **Read-only view:** Ask someone with organization brand-update permission to make the change.
- **Off-brand output:** Confirm the latest generation succeeded, clarify conflicting instructions, and test the worker with a concrete example.

<Warning>
  Upload only materials your organization is authorized to use. Generated
  guidance does not establish ownership of a logo, font, image, or other
  third-party material.
</Warning>
