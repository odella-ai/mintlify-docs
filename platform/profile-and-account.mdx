# Manage your personal account

Open **Account → Profile** to manage your name, profile image, contact verification, and saved signature. Confirm the signed-in identity before making security-sensitive changes.

## Profile and contact details

Update your name or photo using the controls on the page. For email, phone, and other sign-in details, follow the offered account-management and verification flow.

For phone verification, enter the correct country and number, request a code, and complete verification. A personal verified phone number is not a reserved workforce number; see [Phone numbers](/platform/phone-numbers) for worker channels.

## Manage your signature

Select **Manage signature** to view or create a saved signature. Use the offered capture controls, then select **Save signature**. You can later **Change signature** or **Remove signature**.

A saved signature is a personal convenience for signing. Saving it does not sign a document, send invitations, or let an AI Employee sign on your behalf. Removing it does not remove signatures from already completed records.

## Understand the different removal actions

| Action                             | Scope                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Terminate a worker**             | Removes that AI Employee and its related setup.                                             |
| **Remove organization membership** | Changes a person's participation in that organization.                                      |
| **Delete an organization**         | A separate organization lifecycle action requiring appropriate authority.                   |
| **Delete your account**            | Deletes your signed-in user identity after the required checks and organization departures. |

Do not use one action as a substitute for another. Shared organization records are not automatically deleted when an individual user leaves or deletes their account.

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the handover">
    Review your organization memberships and responsibilities. Resolve any
    displayed departure requirements and reassign accountable worker managers or
    other responsibilities where required. Save any personal information you
    need through permitted export/download controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the deletion control">
    In **Account → Profile**, choose **Delete account** and confirm the
    displayed signed-in identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and confirm">
    Complete identity reverification if requested. Type **DELETE** exactly, then
    use the confirmation control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for a confirmed result">
    Successful deletion signs you out. If deletion cannot be confirmed, read the
    error and check your sign-in and membership state before retrying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Account deletion is destructive. It is not a promise that every retained
  record disappears immediately: shared organization data and records subject to
  retention obligations follow their applicable lifecycle and policies.
</Warning>

If deletion stops after some organization departures, review your memberships before trying again. Contact [support@odella.ai](mailto:support@odella.ai) if the result remains uncertain; do not keep repeating a destructive action blindly.
