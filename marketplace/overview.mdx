# Start from proven building blocks

The Marketplace provides ready-to-use AI Employees, workflows, and templates. Use it when a published starting point fits your business need, then review and adapt the item before relying on it for production work.

## Browse the Marketplace

Search by name or purpose, browse business categories, or filter by capability and connected application. Marketplace availability and categories can change as new items are published.

| Item type | What you receive |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Employee** | A ready-to-hire worker with a role and initial configuration. |
| **Workflow** | A reusable process that can be reviewed, tested, and connected to workers. |
| **Template** | A reusable document starting point for consistent drafting. |

## Use a workflow or template

Marketplace workflows and templates are starting points, not immutable packages.

- Review workflow steps, integrations, inputs, outputs, and approval points before publishing.
- Review template fields, instructions, formatting, and sample output before assigning it to workers.
- Confirm that connected applications and source material are appropriate for your organization.

## Marketplace governance

<Warning>
  Installing an item does not replace your organization's review. Validate its configuration, permissions, and outputs before using it for sensitive or business-critical work.
</Warning>

Recommended review questions:

- Who published the item?
- What information and applications can it access?
- Can it send messages or modify external records?
- Where does human approval occur?
- Who owns the process after installation?
- What test confirms that the output is acceptable?
