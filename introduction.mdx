## Find a guide

- [Onboard an AI Employee](/guides/onboard-an-employee): set its role, company context, and working hours.
- [Assign and review work](/guides/assign-and-review-work): chat with a worker, follow progress, and review actions and results.
- [Connect tools and channels](/guides/connect-tools-and-channels): connect workplace applications and choose where people can contact a worker.
- [Schedule recurring work](/guides/automate-recurring-work): repeat a tested task on a schedule or start work from an event.
- [Work with documents and knowledge](/guides/documents-and-knowledge): share source material, create drafts, and collect signatures.
- [Set access and safeguards](/guides/access-and-security): manage who can use a worker, who can edit it, and which actions need review.
- [Manage your account](/guides/manage-your-account): invite colleagues, manage billing, and maintain shared settings.

For specific controls, see [AI Employee settings](/workforce/settings-overview). For common questions, see the [FAQ](/get-started/faq).
