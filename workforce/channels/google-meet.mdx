Google Meet meeting access, saved defaults and live attendance are different capabilities. A calendar/Meet toolkit connection is not proof that the employee can join, record or transcribe a meeting.

## Before you start

For an account where Meet is offered, you need permission to manage the employee's channels, an authorized Google connection for the requested meeting operations, and the host's permission. A meeting link alone does not provide Google API authorization.

Live participation is beta and dial-in based, not general browser/video attendance. It additionally needs a supported authenticated phone/voice path, dial-in details, any required PIN/DTMF support, and account entitlement. Ask support to confirm the supported path before depending on attendance.

## Configure available defaults

1. Open the employee's available Google Meet setup.
2. Enter the **Default Meet URL** for the approved meeting, if needed.
3. Add **Default Dial-in Number** and **Default Meeting PIN** where appropriate. Keep PINs in the intended setup field, not broadly shared notes.
4. Save and complete any connection/publication step actually offered in your account.
5. Ask support or use the available setup/preflight check to verify Google authorization and the chosen participation method. A published record or three filled fields is not sufficient readiness evidence.

The current default-fields guide does not provide a complete Google authorization wizard. If authorization is unavailable, stop and resolve it rather than assuming the saved link grants access.

## Test only in an approved meeting

Confirm date, timezone, host, participants and whether dial-in is available. Ask for a short internal test with a human present. Have the employee disclose that it is an AI Employee, state whether it actually joined, and report any admission/connection failure.

Get the required participant/host consent before recording, transcription or distributing notes. Host admission alone does not authorize those actions. Accessing meeting artifacts depends on Google permissions and on artifacts actually existing; setup cannot create a transcript for a meeting that was never transcribed.

## Troubleshooting and fallback

- **No Meet option:** account/product setup is not exposed; contact support.
- **Authorization check fails:** complete the approved Google connection with sufficient permissions; do not paste raw Google tokens into chat.
- **Link saved but join fails:** check live-join availability, authenticated phone transport, dial-in number/PIN, DTMF support and host admission.
- **No recording/transcript:** verify provider permissions and whether the meeting actually produced that artifact.

Use web chat for a direct conversation with the employee or [Phone](/workforce/channels/phone) for supported telephone calls. Neither automatically enables Google Meet. Live calls and external provider services may incur charges.
