---
title: "Web chat"
description: "Set up an AI Employee’s web conversation, presentation, and optional spoken replies."
---

Web chat is created with your AI Employee. It is different from installing a Slack or Telegram bot, and does not require a provider application or bot token.

## Before you start

- Use the correct personal or organization account and an AI Employee you are allowed to use.
- To edit channel settings, you need permission to manage that employee's channels.
- Check the employee's working hours, availability, and account limits before testing.

## Set up the conversation

1. Open **Workforce**, choose the AI Employee, and open its **Channels** capability.
2. Open **Chat** to edit the existing channel rather than creating a duplicate.
3. Set the **Title**, optional **Subtitle**, **Description**, **Conversation Starters**, and **Disclaimer Message**.
4. If **Voice & Speech** is offered, choose when text-to-speech replies should be attached. The employee's identity settings determine its voice.
5. Select **Save** for an existing published channel. Follow the editor's **Create** control only if the channel is not yet published.
6. Return to the employee and send a low-risk request in its web conversation. Confirm the reply and related Activity.

A saved introduction or conversation starter changes presentation, not the employee's responsibilities, assigned skills, or security policy. Use the relevant employee settings for those changes.

## Spoken replies versus browser voice

Text-to-speech attaches generated audio to a reply. A browser **Talk** conversation, where offered, is a separate live voice experience and may ask for microphone permission. Neither means that a phone number or external meeting channel has been configured.

## Troubleshooting and changes

- **Cannot open or edit:** check the selected account and your employee access/editing permissions.
- **No reply:** check employee availability, working hours, account limits, and Activity before retrying the request.
- **Unexpected greeting or suggestions:** reopen Chat settings and confirm the saved values; change general instructions separately.
- **No audio:** check the channel's speech mode and employee voice, and review browser playback permissions.

Web chat is a default employee channel and cannot be deleted through ordinary channel removal. Do not treat a shared URL as an access grant. Review privacy/security in the employee UI. Work and generated audio can consume account usage.
