---
title: "Employee email"
description: "Find the AI Employee’s generated email address and verify authorized messages and replies."
---

An AI Employee receives a dedicated address for incoming requests and email replies. This is not a connection to your personal Gmail or Outlook mailbox.

## Before you start

You need access to the intended employee. Editing the channel requires channel-management permission. Send the test from the email identity associated with an authorized account member; this channel does not offer unrestricted public inbound email.

## Set up and test

1. Open **Workforce**, choose the employee, then open **Channels → Email**.
2. Copy the address displayed by Odella. It is generated and read-only; do not guess the address or reuse one from another environment.
3. Review **Channel Access** in the employee UI. The email channel is member-restricted; keep security-policy changes in the employee's settings.
4. Save any supported changes and complete the activation control if shown. An existing published address does not need another app or DNS setup from you.
5. From your authorized member email, send a simple request with a distinctive subject. Start without confidential attachments.
6. Confirm receipt in the employee's Activity and check that its reply returns to the correct mailbox/thread.

Replies use the same employee address. Attachments and rich text are supported, subject to processing limits and access rules.

## If the test fails

- **Wrong or missing address:** wait for employee/channel creation to finish, then reopen Email. Contact support if it remains unavailable.
- **No inbound Activity:** confirm the copied address and your sender's account identity. External or unrecognized senders can be refused.
- **Activity exists but no reply:** check availability, working hours, account limits, spam/junk and any pending approval.
- **Need Gmail or Outlook access:** configure an appropriate toolkit/mailbox connection separately; the employee email channel does not grant access to that mailbox.

## Changes and safety

Generated addresses cannot be edited, and default employee email channels cannot be removed through ordinary deletion. Do not publish the address as an unrestricted public support inbox. Review outbound messaging restrictions before asking the employee to email other people. Email work can consume account usage; approval requirements still apply.
