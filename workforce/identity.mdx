**Identity** controls who the worker is, what it is responsible for, and how it should behave.

Open the worker's **Settings** to find:

- **Identity** — profile fields such as name, role, purpose, personality, and voice.
- **General Instructions** — durable guidance the worker should follow across conversations and tasks.
- **Governance**, in organization accounts — accountable manager, access, editing rights, and conversation sharing.

## Profile settings

| Setting              | What it does                                                               | Limits or insight                                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Picture / avatar** | The worker's visual identity.                                              | Used in the app and supported connected channels.                     |
| **Name**             | Display name used in the app and connected channels.                       | 2–255 letters and spaces.                                             |
| **Role**             | Short job label, such as “Payroll Administrator” or “Research Analyst.”    | 2–255 characters.                                                     |
| **Purpose**          | Concise description of the worker's responsibilities and intended outcome. | 2–240 characters. Keep it specific and outcome-focused.               |
| **Personality**      | Behavioural style applied to the worker.                                   | Choose one of the styles below.                                       |
| **Voice**            | Voice used for supported spoken responses.                                 | Available voices are filtered to options compatible with the profile. |

The accountable manager is configured under [Governance](/workforce/governance). This assignment carries worker-management authority; it is not merely a descriptive profile field.

## Personality options

| Personality         | Behaviour                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Default**         | Balanced and adaptive.        |
| **Analytical**      | Precise and methodical.       |
| **Creative**        | Inventive and expressive.     |
| **Empathetic**      | Warm and understanding.       |
| **Assertive**       | Direct and action-oriented.   |
| **Collaborative**   | Team-focused and inclusive.   |
| **Detail-oriented** | Thorough and quality-focused. |
| **Strategic**       | Big-picture systems thinker.  |
| **Supportive**      | Patient and encouraging.      |

## General Instructions

General Instructions are durable working guidance. Use them for rules and preferences that should apply across conversations and tasks, including:

- Tone and writing style
- Escalation rules
- Approval expectations
- Role boundaries
- Actions the worker must never take
- Output formatting
- How to handle uncertainty

General Instructions complement—not replace—Security & Privacy settings. A written instruction cannot grant access to a blocked recipient, tool, or application.

<Tip>
  Make instructions concrete. “Escalate contract variations over $50,000 to the
  manager” is better than “be careful with important contracts.”
</Tip>

## Saving changes

Use **Save Changes** in the relevant settings panel to apply edits or **Cancel** to discard them. Editing rights can differ between Identity and General Instructions; being able to view the worker does not guarantee you can change either.
