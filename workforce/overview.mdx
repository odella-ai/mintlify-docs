---
title: "Hire an AI Employee"
description: "Create a worker for a specific job, review its setup, and get it ready for a first task."
---

Open **Workforce → Add Worker**. Create an AI Employee for your team, or [hire a ready-to-use worker from the Marketplace](/marketplace/overview).

Start with a job you can explain and review—for example, **Supplier proposal reviewer**. You do not need to build a visual workflow first.

## Create a worker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the job">
    Choose the new-worker option. Describe the responsibility or provide supporting material. Include the expected result, what the worker should avoid, and when it should ask a person for help.

    Example: “Review supplier proposals. Summarize price, delivery dates, and missing terms with source references. Return findings for a human to review; do not contact suppliers.”

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the proposed setup">
    Check the name, role, purpose, instructions, and suggested resources. Do not grant access to applications or knowledge just because setup recommends them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm ownership and access">
    In an organization account, assign the accountable manager and review the worker's audience and editors in **Settings → Governance**. Check availability and working hours before assigning work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Create the worker, then add the approved context it needs for the job. Use sample material for the first test, not production credentials or confidential records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Creating the worker manually?">
  Give it a distinct **name**, a short **role**, a one- or two-sentence
  **purpose**, and a recognizable profile image and color. After creation, set
  its durable instructions and review access; a job title alone does not define
  its permissions.
</Accordion>

## Give it its first useful job

1. [Set role and instructions](/workforce/identity): describe what a good result looks like.
2. [Add company knowledge](/workforce/knowledge) and [choose necessary tools](/workforce/tools).
3. [Create a first task](/get-started/quickstart), answer any questions, and inspect the result.

If a worker does not start, check its service state, working hours, permissions, and the stated task blocker. **Stopped** means paused; **Outside working hours** follows the configured after-hours policy. [Working hours and availability](/workforce/settings-operations-logistics) explains those settings.
