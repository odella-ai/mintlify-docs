## The Workforce Overview Page

Search for someone by name, sort the list however you like, or click **Add Worker** when you're ready to bring someone new on board.

Every employee shows up here as a card — photo, name, role, a quick description, and a clock icon telling you where they stand:

- **Stopped** means they haven't been started yet. Hit play to get them going, or open their knowledge library or settings first.
- **Available now** or **Outside working hours** means they're live. From here you can jump straight into chat with them, pause them, or open their knowledge library or settings.

That availability is set on each employee's own profile — see [The Worker Profile](#the-worker-profile) below if you want to change someone's working hours.

## Adding a Worker

Click **Add Worker** on the Workforce Overview page.

  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline preload="auto" controls width="100%" className="dark:hidden" src="../assets/videos/Addingworker.mp4"></video>
  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline preload="auto" controls width="100%" className="hidden dark:block" src="../assets/videos/Addingworker-dark.mp4"></video>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose How to Add a Worker">
    Two cards:

    - **Hire from Marketplace** — browse and hire pre-configured workers; ideal for common roles and quick starts.
    - **Create New Worker** — build a custom AI employee from scratch; best for specialized roles and unique requirements.

    Select one and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Setup Method (Create New Worker path)">
    "How would you like to set up your worker?" Two cards:

    - **Let AI Help Me** — describe the role and Kara sets up the skills, tools, and workflows for you.
    - **I'll Do It Myself** — manually configure the worker step by step.

    Select one and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let AI Help Me — Add the Details">
    "Describe the role or attach a job description and we'll handle the rest."

    - Describe the worker's role in the free-text box, or use one of the starter templates (Payroll Administrator, Recruiting Coordinator, Expense Auditor, Invoice Processor, Document Controller).
    - Optionally attach a job description (PDF, DOC, DOCX, CSV, TXT, or Markdown).
    - Click **Create Worker**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="I'll Do It Myself — Manual Configuration">
    - **Profile Image** — choose an avatar.
    - **Background Color** — choose an avatar background color.
    - Add the details (keep the **Purpose** to 1–2 short sentences):
      - **Name** (e.g., "Kevin")
      - **Role** (e.g., "Report Analyst")
      - **Purpose** (e.g., "Analyzes performance data and produces clear reports for the team…")
    - Click **Create Worker**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hire from Marketplace">
    Clicking **Hire from Marketplace → Next** opens the Marketplace ("Discover Solutions for Every Task"):

    - Search bar for AI employees, templates, or workflows.
    - Content organized by category (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Success), each with a template count.
    - Browse or search, select the AI employee you want, and click **Hire**.

    Marketplace also covers templates and workflows, not only pre-built employees. See [Marketplace Overview](/marketplace/overview) for a full walkthrough of the catalog.

      <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline preload="auto" controls width="100%" className="dark:hidden" src="../assets/videos/Hireagent.mp4"></video>
      <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline preload="auto" controls width="100%" className="hidden dark:block" src="../assets/videos/Hireagent-dark.mp4"></video>
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Kara** is Odella's AI setup assistant, referenced in the "Let AI Help Me" flow and elsewhere in the product (e.g., "Choose what Kara can do…", "Allow Kara to send alerts…").
</Info>

The new employee appears on the **Workforce Overview** page. Click on the employee to configure their skills, connect workflows, and manage settings.

<Tip>
  **Best Practices for Effective AI Employees:**
  - Design clear, specific roles that match real workflow needs
  - Connect relevant knowledge so they can access your firm's expertise
  - Start with simple tasks and expand capabilities over time
  - Use descriptive names that reflect their function in your organization
</Tip>

## Delegating Work to Your AI Employees

Interact with your AI employees the same way you work with your team - no new systems to learn, no complicated interfaces. These connections are configured on the worker's profile, in two places:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Connect an inbox from the **Inboxes** tab via **Connect Inbox**, so the employee can monitor email, keep context, and handle follow-ups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="comments">
    Talk to the employee directly using the **Chat** button on their profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp & More" icon="slack">
    Add channels from the **Add Communication Channels** skill panel — see [The Worker Profile](#the-worker-profile) below.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Microsoft Teams" icon="microsoft">
    Configure alongside your other communication channels from the **Add Communication Channels** skill panel.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
