**What you’ll do:** create a worker to review a supplier proposal, give it sample material, and check its answer. No custom integration or visual workflow is needed for this first exercise.

**Before you start:** sign in to [Odella](https://app.odella.ai), confirm the intended account, and have a non-sensitive sample proposal ready. You need permission to create and use a worker; account funding, limits, and availability may also affect whether work can start.

## Start your first conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hire a worker for one job">
    Open **Workforce → Add Worker**. Create a worker with a role such as **Supplier proposal reviewer**.

    Describe the job: “Review supplier proposals, summarize commercial terms, and flag missing information for a human reviewer.” Keep the responsibility narrow. For detailed creation options, see [Hire an AI Employee](/workforce/overview).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it clear instructions and boundaries">
    Review the worker's role and instructions. Tell it what a useful result looks like, when to ask questions, and which uncertainties to flag.

    In an organization account, assign an accountable manager and review who can use and edit the worker under **Settings → Governance**. Do not connect production tools or confidential knowledge just to complete this exercise.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a chat with sample material">
    Open your worker's profile and select **Chat** where available. Attach the sample proposal in the message composer and wait for the upload to finish. If Chat is unavailable, check the worker's published web channel and your access with its manager. See [Chat with an AI Employee](/workforce/chat).

    Use this request:

    > Review the attached supplier proposal. Return a short summary and a table covering price, delivery dates, renewal terms, and missing information. Cite the source for each finding. Flag anything uncertain. Do not contact the supplier or change external records.

    Instructions guide the work; [access and action policies](/guides/access-and-security) enforce supported restrictions. Send the message and follow the reply in the conversation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow progress and answer questions">
    Answer clarification questions in the conversation. Review any approval request before deciding; do not treat a typed “yes” as a substitute for the approval control. If the worker needs sign-in or a CAPTCHA, use its [browser handoff](/workforce/computer), not a password in chat.

    If work does not start, check the stated blocker, worker availability, permissions, and [account limits](/platform/usage-and-limits). For delegated work, [Tasks](/workforce/tasks) explains status and recovery controls; you do not need to create a duplicate task.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result before expanding the job">
    Open the returned answer and any files. Check that the summary matches the proposal, source references are useful, and missing details are clearly separated from facts.

    Correct unclear instructions and try another representative sample. A completed status is not a substitute for checking the actual result.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## You are ready for the next step when…

- The worker's job and manager are clear.
- You can start a conversation, respond to questions, and find the result.
- The answer meets your review criteria on representative sample material.
- You understand what access you granted—and what you did not grant.
